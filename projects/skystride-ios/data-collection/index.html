Skystride for iPhone · CurrentTech

Data Collection

Effective 30 October 2026

Skystride is developed by Halalisani Mbanjwa at CurrentTech. The game does not send gameplay, device or personal data to developer-operated servers. Progress stays on your device. Optional Game Center features use Apple’s service, and contacting support shares only the information you choose to email.

Stored on your iPhone

  • Records, rank, earned currency, missions and wardrobe
  • Runner and world selections, sound and motion settings
  • Achievement progress and the Daily Sky Trial Echo
  • Photo-mode shots you explicitly save to Photos

Handled by Apple

  • Optional Game Center sign-in
  • Scores and achievement reports when signed in
  • Player identities and leaderboard entries for standings
  • Apple account, service and sharing settings under Apple’s policies

Not collected by CurrentTech from gameplay

  • No gameplay or device analytics sent to a developer server
  • No advertising identifier or cross-app tracking
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera data
  • No access to your existing Photos library

Only if you email support

  • Your email address and message
  • Device or app information you choose to include
  • Any attachments you decide to send
  • Used to respond and resolve your request

Your controls

Manage Game Center sign-in and sharing in iPhone Settings. Photo saving is optional and its add-only permission can be changed in Settings. Deleting Skystride removes local progress, so contact support before reinstalling if you are trying to recover a save.
