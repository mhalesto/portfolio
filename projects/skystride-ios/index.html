Skystride · A fantasy runner for iPhone

Run beyond the clouds.

A portrait 3D endless runner through floating cities, ancient forests and glowing festival nights. Swipe to change lanes, jump and slide, then link aerial tricks and close escapes to build Flow.

01

Six worlds to discover

Explore Sky Arches, Wonderfall, Aethrion, Wonderdark, Reverie and Luminara. Build a Grand Tour through your favourite worlds, with changing landscapes, weather and music.

02

The road comes alive

SKYBREAK events transform a run into an escape across falling bridges, an impossible road or a storm. Midnight Encore brings crimson skies and an extended amber-lit garden.

03

Make every run count

Race your own Echo in the Daily Sky Trial, deliver Sky Courier parcels, complete missions and climb 60 Sky Rank levels. Choose from six runners and earn your wardrobe in play.

04

Chase a new best

Six Game Center leaderboards and 50 achievements celebrate scores, distance, combos and milestones. Personal records remain available when you play offline.

05

Set your own atmosphere

Adjust music, world sounds and effects separately. Calm Motion softens camera movement and decorative effects while preserving the route and its timing.

06

Keep the moment

Pause a run to compose a photo, then choose whether to save it to Photos. Core play works offline, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Your journey stays with you.

Play on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Core play works offline and progress is saved on your device. Game Center adds online leaderboards and achievements when you sign in; it does not back up your local save.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No separate Skystride account required.
