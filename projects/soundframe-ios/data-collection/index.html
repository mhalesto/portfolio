Data Collection

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SoundFrame is designed so core editing workflows can run on device, while optional account, subscription, cloud caption, AI, and advertising attribution features may collect the data needed to provide, secure, measure, and improve those services.

Our goal is to keep collection scoped to what is needed for editing, exports, subscriptions, connected creator tools, support, app reliability, and campaign measurement.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Payment card numbers or full payment credentials
  • Precise location, contacts, or health data
  • Passwords for Apple, Google, or App Store accounts
  • Sale of personal data to data brokers

We avoid collecting data categories that are not required for the app's workflows.

Media and Project Data

Core video, photo, audio, caption and timeline editing is processed on your device. Using cloud captions, AI Director, Product Ad, image or video generation, music videos, 3D creation or other connected tools sends the selected media, sampled frames, audio, prompts, product details, lyrics, transcripts or project context to our backend and the provider needed for that request. Cloud job records, source references and generated outputs may be stored to run or resume work and show your Library. We do not automatically upload your entire Photos library.

A reference photo or selected video frame may contain a face. It is used as creative reference or scene context for the feature you request, rather than to identify a real person. On-device motion tracking can follow a subject for editable effects. These workflows do not create a face-recognition account or identity database.

Account and Diagnostic Data

Optional Apple or Google sign-in can provide your name and email address to authenticate and link your account. Cloud photos/videos, audio, prompts, lyrics and briefs are used for the creation or caption feature you request and can be linked to a guest or linked account ID. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports, non-fatal errors and diagnostic context associated with that ID to improve reliability. These account, media and diagnostic categories are used for app functionality, not by our app for advertising tracking. Existing identifiers, purchase events and product interactions used by the TikTok attribution SDK remain subject to the tracking disclosures below.

Identifiers and App Events

SoundFrame may process user identifiers, device identifiers, subscription status, purchase history, and product interaction events such as app launches, registration, login, tutorial completion, and subscription activity. These records help unlock features, prevent fraud, provide support, measure performance, and understand campaign effectiveness.

Permissions We Use

  • Photos and Media Library: to import media and save finished exports.
  • Speech Recognition: to convert selected audio into timed captions.
  • App Tracking Transparency: to ask before using advertising identifiers for campaign attribution and measurement.

Permissions are requested contextually when you try to use a feature that depends on them.

Payments

Optional subscription billing is processed by Apple. Apple may share purchase status so paid features can be unlocked, but payment credentials are not collected by us.

We only use subscription status information needed to determine whether Pro features are active.

Advertising Attribution and TikTok SDK

SoundFrame may use the TikTok Business SDK to measure app installs, sign-ins, subscriptions, and selected app events from TikTok advertising campaigns. TikTok and SoundFrame may process device advertising identifiers, app or user identifiers, purchase or subscription events, and product interaction events for advertising attribution, analytics, and campaign measurement.

This data may be used for tracking as defined by Apple. On iOS, you can allow or deny tracking when prompted, and you can change that choice later in iOS Settings.

Your Privacy Choices

You can choose local editing without optional AI requests, choose which media to submit, decline tracking, and manage permissions in iOS Settings. Apple and Google linking are optional; the app can use a guest cloud identity. Sign-out does not by itself delete server records. To request access, correction or deletion of your SoundFrame account or cloud data, contact currenttech.co.za@gmail.com and identify the account or creation involved. We may need to verify ownership. Manage subscription cancellation through your Apple account; deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.
