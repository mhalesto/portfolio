CLIPAURA FOR IPHONE

Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

ClipAura is a creative iOS video studio. Ordinary editing, captions, subject cutout, projects, and exports stay on your device. A private Firebase identity stores onboarding choices, and hosted AI receives media only after you approve the request.

On-device creation

ClipAura reads photos and videos only after you grant Photos access. Cutting, transitions, colour filters, captions, subject cutout, timeline editing, and export run on your device. Projects and exported files stay in the app container or in Photos when you choose Save.

Microphone access is used only when you record a voiceover. Speech Recognition access is used for on-device captions. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Private account and cloud profile

ClipAura automatically creates a private Firebase guest identity so onboarding choices can be saved securely. The cloud profile can include a Firebase user identifier, role, interests, style choices, onboarding progress, and an optional display name.

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you choose it, Firebase Authentication may receive the private relay or other email address Apple provides, along with the name you choose to share. Apple sign-in upgrades the guest identity so the profile can be restored on another device.

Hosted AI

Before a hosted AI request, ClipAura explains what will be shared and asks for your permission. If you agree, your prompt and selected reference image or video frame are sent through ClipAura's secured Firebase service to BytePlus Seedance 2.5.

The service stores the account identifier, model task identifier, timestamps, task status, and daily usage count so it can return the result, resume pending work, and control abuse. Do not submit media you do not have permission to share.

Payments

Apple processes subscriptions and payment details. ClipAura receives StoreKit product and entitlement information but never receives your full payment-card details. Subscriptions can be restored in the app and managed in your Apple Account settings.

Tracking and advertising

ClipAura does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not use the advertising identifier.

Retention and deletion

Local projects remain until you delete them or remove the app. The cloud profile and AI task metadata remain while the private account exists.

Open Profile, then Account, then Delete ClipAura account to permanently delete the Firebase identity, cloud profile, AI usage, and AI task history. Local projects remain on that device so account deletion does not unexpectedly destroy personal files. App Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Apple's subscription settings.

Children

ClipAura is a general-audience creative tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from a child without the permission required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

For privacy, account-deletion, purchase, or technical questions, email currenttech.co.za@gmail.com. Never send passwords, Apple verification codes, or payment-card details.
