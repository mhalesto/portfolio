RESUMESTUDIO FOR IPHONE AND IPAD

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

ResumeStudio is an offline-first career workspace. Your drafts and career records stay on your device by default. Optional sync, connected AI, live spoken interviews and hosted sharing have different data flows, described below, and run when you choose those features.

Data stored on your device

Your device stores résumé documents and photos, versions, layouts, cover letters, applications, selected contacts, offers, interview history, career evidence and attached source files, imported inbox items, AI results, coach conversations and remembered context, Writing Voice Profile samples, preferences and verified offline purchase access.

Importing a selected contact does not upload your entire address book. Messages, screenshots, documents and calendar invitations shared into the Application Inbox are analyzed locally for your review before any proposed workspace change is applied.

On-device intelligence and OCR

Apple Vision can read selected images, screenshots and scanned documents on your device. On supported Apple Intelligence devices, suitable lightweight tasks may use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Content handled entirely on device is not sent to ResumeStudio’s backend or OpenAI.

Availability depends on the device and system settings. Connected fallback is a separate setting and may use the credits shown for the action. You can pause AI in Privacy Centre. A local OCR preview and a connected AI import are separate choices.

Connected writing and Career Coach

Requested connected writing, tailoring, interview planning, feedback and coach actions send the relevant context through ResumeStudio’s Firebase backend to OpenAI. Dedicated names, phone numbers, email addresses, references and private source-URL fields are omitted from ordinary résumé-writing context. Private photos, signatures, attachment files and reference details are excluded from the redacted page image used for requested coach layout feedback. Text you place in a job description, note or chat may still contain personal information; review it before sending.

The coach can read relevant saved work through named tools. Selected relationship roles, companies, follow-ups and notes may be included when relevant; requested negotiation help can include the compensation and offer amounts you entered. Résumé, application, answer-vault and reminder changes proposed by the coach require your approval.

Coach memories are stored on device and can be reviewed, kept or forgotten; relevant remembered context may be sent with a coach request. The Writing Voice Profile sends a short style brief with tone, length, spelling and preferred or banned phrases. Its saved writing examples remain on device.

AI résumé import

If you choose connected AI import, the extracted text of the document is sent for structured parsing. That text can contain your name, contact details, employment history and other personal information in the original résumé; ordinary writing redaction does not apply to this import step. The original image or document file is not uploaded for ordinary OCR. Review the proposed imported fields before applying them. Saved AI output can be stored under your Firebase account.

Recorded answer practice

Single-answer voice practice records to your device. Speech recognition prefers on-device recognition where supported and may use Apple’s speech service when local recognition is unavailable. Choosing connected AI feedback sends the transcript and delivery measurements, rather than the saved audio file, to the writing service.

Spoken mock interviews

Starting a live spoken interview sends microphone audio directly from your device to OpenAI’s realtime service. ResumeStudio’s Firebase backend issues a short-lived session credential; it does not relay or store the live audio. The transcript is saved on device and can be sent later if you request connected feedback or a debrief.

OpenAI’s provider retention rules apply to live audio and transcripts. ResumeStudio does not promise zero-retention processing. You can stop the session to stop streaming microphone audio.

Provider processing and retention

ResumeStudio’s text requests disable stored API responses. This does not remove OpenAI’s separate security and abuse-monitoring retention. Under OpenAI’s default API controls, request content may be retained for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its policy. Approved retention controls have not been verified for these sessions. API data is not used for model training by default. See OpenAI’s API data controls at https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data.

iCloud, accounts and service identifiers

If you enable iCloud sync, the workspace archive and associated evidence files are stored in your private iCloud container under your Apple Account. Apple operates that service.

When online, ResumeStudio creates a protected anonymous Firebase account so supported services can work without mandatory signup. Optional email or Sign in with Apple adds account information. Firebase can retain your account identifier, saved AI results, allowance and referral records, signed purchase-verification information, hosted content and its ownership metadata. An installation identifier is sent with service requests and may be hashed for allowance enforcement; it is not an advertising identifier.

Review Rooms

Publishing a Review Room uploads the selected PDF and rendered page images, together with the room title, message and reviewer context you provide. The PDF may contain the personal information you chose to include. The room uses an unguessable link and access code, expires within 31 days, and can be revoked or deleted by its owner.

Reviewer names, comments, exact suggested edits and sign-off are stored with the room. Suggested edits change your résumé only after you accept them. Deleting the hosted room removes its document, page images and stored feedback.

Trackable résumé links and public CV pages

A trackable link uploads your selected PDF and page images. Its viewer uses an anonymous per-link visitor cookie and records opens, reading time while the page is visible, PDF downloads and a coarse device/browser hint. This retained engagement data is visible to the sender; the viewer is told about it. ResumeStudio does not identify the visitor by name.

A public CV page contains the résumé and public profile fields you choose to publish. It can be search-indexable when you enable that choice. Owners can withdraw, revoke or delete their hosted pages and links. Removing access does not erase copies a recipient has already downloaded.

Career Proof Packs

Publishing a Proof Pack uploads its rendered PDF and page images. Before publishing, you choose the evidence and the redactions for employer names, sources, exact dates, attachment previews and referee names. Included attachment previews and personal details become part of the published document; the underlying source files are not uploaded separately.

A Proof Pack uses an unlisted, private-by-link address without viewer analytics. ResumeStudio does not record Proof Pack opens or reading time for its owner. Packs expire after fourteen days by default and no more than 31 days, use no-index headers, and can be revoked or deleted by their owner. Deleting a pack removes its hosted PDF and page images.

Evidence confirmations and outcome benchmarks

If you request confirmation of one evidence claim, the selected claim and context are hosted behind a one-use link. A referee independently provides their name, role, decision and comment. ResumeStudio does not upload a recipient email or address-book list for this request, and does not verify the identity of the person holding the link. An answered confirmation cannot be edited through ordinary revocation; account erasure removes the owned record.

Outcome benchmarks are a separate opt-in feature. Contributions contain bounded outcome counts and broad role, seniority and market categories rather than résumé text, an employer or a job title. A hashed installation/cohort key replaces a repeat contribution, and a cohort is not published until enough separate contributors are present.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payment details. ResumeStudio receives signed transaction information such as product identifier, status, purchase date and expiration date to unlock access and enforce allowances. ResumeStudio does not receive your payment-card or bank details.

Privacy-safe product measurement

Anonymous product insights are off by default. If you opt in, the app sends only allow-listed event names, plan tier, AI route, app version and a coarse onboarding goal. Events exclude résumé text, account and installation identifiers, contact details, company names, job descriptions, URLs and advertising identifiers. The backend folds events into aggregate daily counters rather than a per-user activity timeline. Those aggregate records expire after 90 days. Turning insights off stops new submissions; existing anonymous totals are not per-user records.

Data sharing and tracking

ResumeStudio does not sell personal data, display third-party advertising or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. Apple, Google/Firebase and OpenAI receive data for the app features described above. Trackable-link engagement is separate from advertising tracking; Proof Packs have no owner-facing viewer analytics.

Retention and deletion

On-device work remains until you erase it or remove the app. iCloud data remains until you erase the archive. Saved AI work remains in Firebase until you delete it or your account. Hosted access ends on expiry, withdrawal, revocation or deletion; expiry ends access and does not by itself promise immediate physical deletion of every hosted object.

Settings → Account and backup provides separate server-account, local-workspace and iCloud controls. Server-account deletion removes the managed account records, saved AI output, hosted CV pages, résumé links, Review Rooms, Proof Packs and owned evidence confirmations. Local erasure and iCloud erasure are separate choices. Provider security-retention rules may still apply to requests already processed, and downloaded copies remain with their recipients. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Children, changes and contact

ResumeStudio is intended for career planning and is not directed to children. Check its current App Store age rating. This policy will be updated before material processing changes take effect.
