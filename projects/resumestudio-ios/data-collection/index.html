RESUMESTUDIO FOR IPHONE AND IPAD

Data Collection

ResumeStudio is an offline-first career workspace. Your drafts and career records stay on your device by default. Optional sync, connected AI, live spoken interviews and hosted sharing have different data flows, described below, and run when you choose those features.

Not requested

  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking.
  • Payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Background location or a general email-inbox feed.
  • A bulk upload of your address book.

Stored on device first

  • Résumés, photos, layouts, versions and exports.
  • Applications, selected contacts, offers, career evidence and attached source files.
  • Imported inbox items, interview records, coach conversations and remembered context.
  • Recorded answer-practice files, transcripts and Writing Voice Profile examples.

Local processing

  • Apple Vision reads selected screenshots and scanned documents locally.
  • Eligible lightweight tasks may use Apple Foundation Models on supported devices.
  • Shared application messages and invites are analyzed locally for review.
  • Apple speech recognition prefers local processing and may use Apple’s speech service when unavailable.

Requested connected processing

  • Ordinary writing sends limited redacted context through Firebase to OpenAI.
  • AI résumé import sends extracted original text, which may include names and contact details.
  • Live spoken interviews stream microphone audio directly to OpenAI; provider retention applies.
  • Relevant coach context can include selected relationship notes and compensation amounts entered for negotiation help.

Hosted sharing you choose

  • Review Rooms host the selected PDF, page images and reviewer feedback behind a link and code.
  • Trackable links retain opens, visible reading time, downloads and a per-link cookie for sender analytics.
  • Public CV pages host selected documents and fields and may be search-indexable.
  • Proof Packs host the reviewed, optionally redacted PDF and page images without owner-facing viewer tracking.
  • Evidence-confirmation links host the selected claim/context and the referee’s own reply.

Accounts, sync and purchases

  • Firebase retains the guest or signed-in account, saved AI results, service identifiers, allowances and ownership records.
  • Apple returns signed purchase status; ResumeStudio receives no payment-card or bank details.
  • The private iCloud workspace and evidence files sync only when you enable sync.
  • Server account, local workspace and iCloud erasure are separate controls; account deletion does not cancel subscriptions.

Optional product measurement

Anonymous product insights are off by default. Opting in sends only coarse allow-listed event, plan, AI route, version and onboarding-goal values. Identity, account/installation identifiers, content and URLs are excluded; daily aggregate records expire after 90 days. Opting out stops future submissions. Outcome benchmarks are a separate opt-in feature with bounded counts and broad categories.
