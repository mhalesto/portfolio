OFFICIAL RESUMESTUDIO SUPPORT

How can we help?

Get help with documents, accounts, purchases, AI credits, recorded and live interview practice, iCloud sync, public CV pages, trackable links, Review Rooms and Proof Packs.

Email support

Include your device model, iOS version, ResumeStudio version and build number, the feature you were using, the exact steps, and the error shown. Attach a screenshot only after checking it contains no résumé or job-search data. Never send passwords, access codes, payment-card details, or a résumé unless you deliberately choose to share it for support.

Account and data

Use Settings → Account and backup to reset a password or separately erase your server account, local workspace and iCloud archive. Server-account deletion also removes owned hosted documents, feedback and evidence confirmations. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Plans and purchases

Use Settings → Plans and purchases to restore purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings. Apple processes payment details; do not send card or bank information to support.

Offline access

Editing, preview, export, applications, saved interview work, OCR and supported on-device AI can work offline. Connected AI, live spoken interviews, account changes and hosted features require a connection.

AI import and writing

Local OCR previews and connected AI import are separate choices. AI import sends extracted document text, including contact details present in the original; ordinary writing uses limited redacted context. Review imported fields and proposed edits before applying them. Privacy Centre lets you pause AI.

Voice and spoken interviews

Recorded answer practice keeps the saved audio file on device; speech recognition may use Apple’s service when local recognition is unavailable. Live spoken interviews stream microphone audio directly to OpenAI and are subject to provider retention. Stop the session to stop audio streaming; only request a debrief when you want to send the transcript for feedback.

Hosted links and privacy

Manage CV pages, trackable links, Review Rooms and Proof Packs from their screens to withdraw, revoke or delete access. Trackable links show sender engagement; Proof Packs do not record owner-facing viewer analytics. A downloaded copy remains with its recipient.

Insights and history

Anonymous product insights are off by default and can be turned off in Privacy Centre. The optional daily totals contain no account or installation identifier and expire after 90 days. Review or forget coach memories and use the separate local, server and iCloud erasure controls for your saved work.
