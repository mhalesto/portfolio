Skystride for iPhone · CurrentTech

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Skystride is developed by Halalisani Mbanjwa at CurrentTech. The game does not send gameplay, device or personal data to developer-operated servers. Progress stays on your device. Optional Game Center features use Apple’s service, and contacting support shares only the information you choose to email.

Progress stays on your device

Your records, earned coins and gems, rank, achievement progress, missions, wardrobe, selected worlds and runners, settings, and Daily Sky Trial Echo are stored locally in the app. Skystride has no developer account system or cloud-save service. Game Center scores are not a backup of your local progress.

Deleting the app removes its local save. Offloading and restoring a device are controlled by iOS; do not rely on Game Center to recover coins, wardrobe or run progress. Photos you save remain in your Photos library until you delete them there.

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Skystride sends scores and achievement progress to Apple and requests leaderboard entries so it can display standings and identify your player row. Apple’s service provides player identifiers and display names for this feature. Your Game Center name and scores may be visible to other players through leaderboards.

Game Center is optional for core play. Manage sign-in and sharing in your iPhone’s Game Center settings. Apple handles its service under its own privacy policy; Skystride does not copy Game Center account information into a developer-run database.

Read Apple’s Game Center & Privacy information

Photos permission

Photo mode asks for permission to add a shot to Photos only when you choose to save. Skystride does not read or scan your photo library and does not upload your shots. You can decline or change this permission in iOS Settings and continue playing.

Advertising, purchases and measurement

Skystride contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, tracking system or in-app purchases. It does not request advertising identifiers, location, contacts, microphone or camera access. No gameplay analytics are sent to CurrentTech. Apple may process App Store or Game Center information under its own policies and your Apple settings.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever message, device details or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your question and resolve the issue. Email is handled by our email provider, and we do not sell support correspondence or use it for advertising.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related support or legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your support correspondence by emailing currenttech.co.za@gmail.com. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Your choices and questions

You can play without Game Center, adjust Game Center sharing through Apple, decline photo-saving access, or delete the app’s local data by deleting the app. We cannot restore or remotely delete a save that exists only on your device.

This policy applies to the Skystride iPhone app. We will update this page if its data handling changes. For privacy questions, contact currenttech.co.za@gmail.com.
